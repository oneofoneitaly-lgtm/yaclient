FAQ

Is YA CLIENT free?
Yes. The launcher and all the mods are free.
Which Minecraft version does it support?
Minecraft 1.8.9 with Forge. The launcher installs everything for you.
Does it work on Mac or Linux?
Not right now. YA CLIENT is Windows only.
Can I play without a premium account?
Yes. You can enter a nickname and play offline. Premium servers need a Microsoft account.
Is it allowed on servers?
YA CLIENT is made for visuals, interface and utility, with no cheat features. Rules change from server to server, so if you are unsure, ask the server staff before joining.
Why does Windows warn me about the file?
New programs without a paid publisher certificate are flagged as unknown. Click More info, then Run anyway.
How do updates work?
The launcher checks for a new version every time you open it and installs it by itself. Mods update when you press Launch Game.
Can I use my own mods?
Yes. Open the Mods page in the launcher and press Open mods folder.
Something is broken. What do I do?
Open the Console page in the launcher, copy the log and send it to us on Discord.
